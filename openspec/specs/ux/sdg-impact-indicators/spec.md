# Capability: SDG Impact Indicators

## Purpose
Displays quantified Sustainable Development Goals (SDG / ODS 2, 12, 13) and farmer economic ROI indicators on the landing page and dashboard.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Quantified SDG and Economic Impact Cards
The system SHALL present structured impact metrics detailing alignment with ODS 2 (Hambre Cero), ODS 12 (Producción Responsable), and ODS 13 (Acción por el Clima).

#### Scenario: Reviewing Environmental and Economic Metrics
- **WHEN** user views the impact section in the platform
- **THEN** the system displays specific indicators: \$0 diagnostic barrier (down from \$150), +40% fertilizer absorption efficiency, up to 3.85 tCO2e/ha/year carbon sequestration potential, and zero-deforestation compliance under the Orinoco Conservation Shield.
