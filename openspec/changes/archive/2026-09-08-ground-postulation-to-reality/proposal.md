## Why

Agrotech Venezuela was created and engineered by Frank Alfonso Sousa Mota as an independent, individual Software Engineer (UNERG 2025 graduate) without institutional backing or agricultural research affiliation. The previous project documentation and postulation dossier framed the work under overambitious assertions: claiming TRL 6 (field-demonstrated system in operating environments), referring to Turén and Calabozo as already-harvested field trials, presenting economic ROI (3.8x) and carbon sequestration as established past achievements, claiming dual categories simultaneously, and denominating the core text as an experimental scientific article.

These overextensions expose the author to technical vulnerability during evaluation and do not represent the true, impressive achievement of the project: a fully functional, open-source WebGIS digital twin with 233 passing automated tests and mathematical algorithm implementations developed in local environment. Grounding the postulation in reality by adopting an **Artículo Técnico** in **Categoría General**, adjusting the maturity to **TRL 4** with a clear roadmap, and articulating case studies and ROI as **computational simulation scenarios and prospective modeling** makes the proposal 100% honest, credible, academically rigorous, and comfortably defensible.

## What Changes

- **Document Typology**: Reframe the core submission from "Artículo Científico" to **"Artículo Técnico"** (focused on software engineering, WebGIS architecture, MapBiomas 40-year data pipeline, and algorithmic modeling), adhering strictly to official MapBiomas bases (Bases 3.1 & 3.11.1).
- **Single Official Category**: Formalize postulation exclusively in **"Categoría General"**, eliminating conflicting references to simultaneous category submissions.
- **Technology Readiness Level (TRL)**: Calibrate official maturity from TRL 6 down to **TRL 4** (*Prototipo Funcional de Software Validado en Entorno de Laboratorio / Desarrollo Local mediante 233 Pruebas Automatizadas*), adding an explicit development roadmap towards TRL 5 (cloud pilot) and TRL 6 (participatory field trials).
- **Case Studies Reframing**: Transform Turén (maize) and Calabozo (rice) narratives from claimed empirical field trials to **"Escenarios de Simulación Computacional y Modelado Prescriptivo"** based on historical MapBiomas series and regional soil equations (Kamprath, Shoelace, Saxton-Rawls).
- **Prospective ROI & Carbon Modeling**: Articulate economic return (ROI) and carbon sequestration (IPCC Tier 2) in conditional and future terms as **algorithmic projection tools** designed to assist farmers, rather than measured past results.
- **Author Identity & Tone**: Consolidate the voice of Frank Sousa as an individual Software Engineer and independent developer applying computing to Venezuelan agriculture.
- **Dossier & PDF Recompilation**: Synchronize all 6 official documents in `docs/mapbiomas_premio_2026/` and `public/docs/`, update UI badges in `/dashboard/postulacion`, and recompile all publication-ready PDFs.
